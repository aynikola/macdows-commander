The taste of a sentence

A good sentence need not speak in bold. Sometimes a whisper in italics will do, or a single underlined word. There is inline code for those coming from the terminal, and a link for when you need to point elsewhere.

Why do we write?

  • To understand
  • So someone may read it one day
